# CUHK Business School AI Hub

**Research · Teaching · Entrepreneurship**

The CUHK Business School AI Hub is an AI-native platform and ecosystem that turns dispersed AI needs into durable institutional capabilities. It connects computing infrastructure, tools, research support, education assets, industry collaboration, and entrepreneurship pathways to deliver measurable AI × business outcomes.

Research, teaching, and practice form a closed loop: research creates new methods and evidence; teaching and corporate training turn them into reusable capabilities; and entrepreneurship and industry collaboration put them to work on real business problems.

## What the AI Hub Includes

- **Shared Capability Base** — HPC and GPU infrastructure, APIs, reusable toolkits, data resources, and technical support.
- **Area I: AI × Business Research** — Compute and engineering support, research workflows, experimentation, project matching, and reusable research assets.
- **Area II: Teaching & Corporate Training** — AI-native courses, teaching materials, corporate training, workflow redesign, and industry collaboration.
- **Area III: OPC & Entrepreneurship** — Idea validation, prototype development, deployment pathways, commercial judgement, and ecosystem connections.

Research projects draw on the shared compute, tooling, and workflow capabilities above. Dedicated research repositories will be added here as they are released.

### Area II: Teaching & Corporate Training

- [`Quaestio-Socratica`](https://github.com/CUHK-Business-School-AI-Hub/Quaestio-Socratica) — Turns source materials or a learning goal into a checkpoint-based, personalized Socratic course.
- [`waymark`](https://github.com/CUHK-Business-School-AI-Hub/waymark) — A derivative of the famous skill `grill-me`. Comparing with the orginal one, waymark is more friendly for non-tech staff and entrepreneurs.
- [`SME_training`](https://github.com/CUHK-Business-School-AI-Hub/SME_training) — Training materials for corporate programmes on agent-native transformation. *(Restricted access.)*
- [`Ronghua`](https://github.com/CUHK-Business-School-AI-Hub/Ronghua) — An industry collaboration project for AI-assisted legal files review. *(Restricted access.)*
- [`TaoYuanWeiYe`](https://github.com/CUHK-Business-School-AI-Hub/TaoYuanWeiYe) — A replenishment system developed with a cross-border e-commerce business. *(Restricted access.)*
- [`BRICK`](https://github.com/CUHK-Business-School-AI-Hub/BRICK) — A reusable template for building general-purpose agent work modules. *(Restricted access.)*

### Area III: OPC & Entrepreneurship

- [`coding_agent_constitution`](https://github.com/CUHK-Business-School-AI-Hub/coding_agent_constitution) — Turns a product idea into durable specifications, architecture, rules, contracts, and bounded implementation tasks.
- [`coding_agent_autoQA`](https://github.com/CUHK-Business-School-AI-Hub/coding_agent_autoQA) — Adds evidence-driven quality assurance, test traceability, review gates, and release-readiness checks to coding-agent projects.
